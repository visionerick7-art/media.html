The ISRLATA MEDIA Principles

The foundation of the network's operations.

1. African originality

Promote original African ideas, stories, languages, culture and creativity.

2. Integrity and authenticity

No purchased engagement, fake views, fake followers, bots or misleading reporting.

3. Creator ownership

Members retain ownership of their personal accounts and original work unless a separate written agreement provides otherwise.

4. Responsible account permissions

Never request or store a member's account password or verification code. Use only authorised platform permissions.

5. Transparent commercial activity

Record actual income, expenses, agreed allocations and payments. Individual channel income remains with its owner unless separately agreed.

6. Sustainable production

Use media to educate, entertain, document and support practical development.
